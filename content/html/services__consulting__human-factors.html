Overview

Combining expertise from ergonomics, psychology, physiology, sociology, anthropometrics and biomechanics our team provides both new build and in-service human factors support to enhance health, safety, usability, performance, and user experience. 

From as early as the Concept and through to Disposal we provide: 

​Design support and assurance (including assurance of the new human factors class rules), and 

Operational support – improving safety and performance through human-centred analysis.

With ever increasing focus on cost-efficiency, sophistication of complex systems and rapid developments in advanced automation and autonomous technologies, our human factors and working environment experts assist you to optimise health, safety, and human performance.
