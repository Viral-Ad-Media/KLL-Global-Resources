IRIS PROJECT MANAGEMENT PLATFORM

Bring your offshore projects into focus.

IRIS delivers a clear, real-time view of project status and progress, helping you optimally manage offshore operations.

Overview

Whether it’s oil and gas platform maintenance or a windfarm installation, it is vital to keep crews and assets safe, productive and under complete control. Our IRIS project management platform makes your remote offshore operations visible. It brings a wealth of reference data together with your own information to give the most complete picture of every project.

What we offer

IRIS’s browser-accessed portal securely gathers and stores all your current and historical project data. From vessel tracking to weather forecasts, its real-time reports and dashboards keep your finger on your project’s pulse.

What are the benefits?

Global marine mapping compiled from over 600 data sets lies at IRIS’s heart, supporting live vessel tracking for marine coordination. There’s rich global metocean data – current observations, marine forecasts and a decade’s worth of legacy information. IRIS takes care of your information – updating and rebuilding reference data nightly.

IRIS absorbs and displays streams of client data from asset sensors, whether it’s helicopter tracking, kilometres surveyed or the speed of a cable trencher. Store and access anything from subsea infrastructure CAD files to pipeline inspection videos in the open database.

As your project data accumulates, analyse and report to build a clear picture of HSEQ statistics, costs and other performance metrics. By understanding what’s happened – and what’s happening right now – IRIS helps you see how to safeguard operations and make accurate and efficient decisions.
