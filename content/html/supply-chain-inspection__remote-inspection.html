Introduction to Remote Inspection

Our remote inspections are designed to provide you with the same level of technical expertise and insight in a safe, flexible and efficient way. With the ability to stream high-quality data with smart cameras, mobile technology or drones meaning that real-time collaboration is now possible – wherever and whenever you need us.

How does it work?

Enabling smarter, safer and more sustainable solutions, our remote inspections can be delivered in a variety of ways including:

Customer-operated:

Our technical expert will guide you through the process while you operate the equipment and we will ensure that you have everything you need for the inspection.

Co-witnessed:

Our technical expert will live-stream the inspection directly to relevant parties within your organisation. Your own stakeholders can co-witness and interact during our inspection whilst keeping your travel costs to a minimum.

Whichever model of delivery you choose, the entire inspection can be documented via visual recording thereby delivering transparency. Pre-agreed data can also be sent to KLL for review and analysis at a later stage for both client-operated and co-witnessed inspections.

Our remote inspections are underpinned with our easy-to-use app, KLL Remote, which is downloadable free of charge from Google Play or the App Store. It can easily be installed across standard mobile devices and only needs internet connectivity either via WiFi or your local network provider (3/4G).

What are the benefits?

Remove geographical boundaries 

Our remote service means that our inspections can easily be delivered directly to you, whatever the circumstance. We put our expertise where it’s needed at the right time – wherever you are in the world. 

Save time, money and travel

Our range of real-time inspection services are delivered straight to your device thereby cutting any wait-times, increasing operational uptime and reducing the cost of travel.

Reduce your organisational carbon footprint

LR Remote enables you to reduce your organisational carbon footprint by reducing the need for travel in relation to your assessment programme.

Identify and mitigate risk in real-time

Real-time co-witnessed or customer operated inspections deliver immediate transparency to your operations worldwide. You can quickly adjust the scope to deep dive into any operational issues and manage the risk of sending workers to hazardous sites and locations, thereby mitigating any risk to your business.
