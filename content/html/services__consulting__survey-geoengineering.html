Overview

At KLL we understand that for offshore engineering developments or operations to run smoothly this must start at the seabed and in the shallow sub-seabed foundation zone. If seabed and shallow subsurface information is not accurate and comprehensive, or is not used effectively or efficiently, this can lead to huge delays in projects, or at worst, cancelled projects.

We apply a true understanding of the value of knowledge derived from seabed and subsurface surveys, and other geospatial data acquisition, and how best to process, interpret, use and share such data, ensuring your project team has a fully comprehensive knowledge of these key project factors, helping you to make the most informed decisions.
