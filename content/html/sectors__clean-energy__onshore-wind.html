Onshore wind has proven itself to be a clean and productive source of energy generation. LR supports the development and operation of onshore wind projects across the global sector, applying experience and expertise from mature markets to those which are at an earlier stage of development. We solve complex problems for onshore wind investors, developers and operators of onshore wind farms. With our broad range of expertise, we can reduce technical, operational and commercial risks while enhancing asset performance. Our services include Site assessment, Energy resource assessment, Risk management, Grid connections, Performance optimisation, Owner’s engineer, Technology qualification and Technical Adjudication.
