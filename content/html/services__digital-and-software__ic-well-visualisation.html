What we offer

Our modular IC software gives stratigraphers, geologists, petrophysicists, engineers and data managers a new view of their subsurface oil and gas data. Bringing all your data together, with tools to analyse it and visualise what it’s telling you.

What are the benefits?

With its robust, open database, IC can import data from anywhere, then combine and share it with ease. Intelligent well correlation, 2D display options, and cross-section building – IC has tools to tackle every analytic challenge. Even with missing information or unconformities, IC produces coherent results. As the only stratographically-aware software, you can incorporate geological time into interpretations through age/depth maps and stratigraphic hierarchies and sequences. There’s powerful interactive mapping, charting and collaboration tools to tap too. Produce and share time-varying grids, live map montages and well path projections. Add production data to IC and zoom out to analyse the whole reservoir, predicting and monitoring well performance over time.
